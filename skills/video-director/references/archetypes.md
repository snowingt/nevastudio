# The 10 Video Director Archetypes

The Video Director structures every video according to one of 10 proven retention archetypes. Each archetype specifies the timeline architecture, narrative pacing, camera choreography, and sound design.

---

## 01. The Viral Hook (Scroll-Stopper)
- **Primary Goal**: Stop the thumb within 1.5s, create an irresistible curiosity loop, maximize 15-second completion rate.
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 1.5s`: **The Pattern Interrupt**. Visual anomaly (physics break, scale warp, or kinetic text slam) + audio impact.
  - `1.5s – 4.0s`: **Curiosity Confirmation**. Introduce the central contradiction or stakes ("Why does this happen?").
  - `4.0s – 11.0s`: **The Rapid Escalation**. Fast-paced demonstration or micro-revelations every 2 seconds.
  - `11.0s – 15.0s`: **Payoff & Loop Setup**. Resolution connects back to the opening frame for an infinite loop.
- **Recommended Engine**: `hand-drawn-canvas-animation`, `kinetic`, `remotion-motion-graphics`.

---

## 02. The SaaS & Tech Product Launch
- **Primary Goal**: Make software look like an Apple Keynote reveal; transform abstract code/UI into tangible cinematic desire.
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 2.0s`: **The Problem Chaos Flash**. Red error alerts, messy spreadsheets, or slow loading spinners (0.3s cuts).
  - `2.0s – 5.0s`: **The Hero Float**. 3D angled floating device materializes out of the dark void with subtle glow.
  - `5.0s – 10.0s`: **The One-Click Magic**. Cursor executes single click; wave of order sweeps across the interface.
  - `10.0s – 15.0s`: **The Metric Flex & Call-To-Action**. Rapid counter ticks to 10x ROI; clean logo badge and CTA.
- **Recommended Engine**: `remotion-dev` / `remotion-motion-graphics`, `hyperframes`.

---

## 03. The Personal Brand & Founder Authority
- **Primary Goal**: Build instant thought-leader credibility; deliver high-signal insight with zero fluff.
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 2.0s`: **Contrarian Truth Hook**. Bold statement challenging standard industry advice + tight punch-in.
  - `2.0s – 6.0s`: **The Evidence / The Framework**. Visual diagram or kinetic breakdown supporting the thesis.
  - `6.0s – 12.0s`: **The Practical Execution**. Step-by-step actionable rule (Step 1, Step 2).
  - `12.0s – 15.0s`: **The Closing Razor**. Punchy one-sentence summary to save/bookmark.
- **Recommended Engine**: `video-edit`, `kinetic`, `remotion-motion-graphics`.

---

## 04. The Course & Educational Promo
- **Primary Goal**: Turn complex knowledge into a modular, achievable roadmap; sell transformation, not hours of video.
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 2.0s`: **The Pain Point Flash**. "Stop wasting months doing X..."
  - `2.0s – 7.0s`: **The Modular Roadmap**. Visualizing the 3 core pillars as floating technical badges/cards.
  - `7.0s – 12.0s`: **The Student Outcome**. Before/After skill transformation or live workflow demo.
  - `12.0s – 15.0s`: **Enrollment Action**. Limited access badge and clear link trigger.
- **Recommended Engine**: `hand-drawn-canvas-animation`, `animate`, `explicador-animado`.

---

## 05. The Faceless Conceptual Channel
- **Primary Goal**: Hypnotic, high-retention storytelling without any human on camera; relies on metaphors and motion continuity.
- **Three Golden Rules**:
  1. *Never let the screen rest*: Every single second has camera drift, particle flow, or dimensional shifting.
  2. *First frame is the thumbnail*: Must be arresting and self-explanatory in silence.
  3. *Lighting is character*: Emotional tone conveyed through color temperature and contrast.
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 2.0s`: Visual metaphor (burning currency, melting ice, ticking pressure gauge).
  - `2.0s – 8.0s`: Internal mechanism cutaway / animated blueprint flow.
  - `8.0s – 14.0s`: Deep causal explanation with animated labels and arrows.
  - `14.0s – 15.0s`: Final synthesis diagram.
- **Recommended Engine**: `hand-drawn-canvas-animation`, `animate`, `moneyprinterturbo-core`.

---

## 06. The Luxury & High Aesthetic
- **Primary Goal**: Convey premium exclusivity, craft, and sophistication through extreme restraint.
- **Rules of Luxury**:
  - Minimal color palette: Monochromatic, deep obsidian black, platinum white, brushed gold accent.
  - Camera: Strictly slow, deliberate push-ins and orbits. Never whip pan or snap zoom.
  - Typography: Elegant serif or clean geometric grotesque with wide letter-spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.15em`).
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 3.0s`: Macro silhouette, gentle light sweep revealing pure texture.
  - `3.0s – 9.0s`: Deliberate camera glide highlighting architectural lines.
  - `9.0s – 15.0s`: Subtle brand mark reveal with sub-bass acoustic resolution.
- **Recommended Engine**: `remotion-motion-graphics`, `hyperframes`, `google-flow-automation`.

---

## 07. The Before & After (Transformation Arc)
- **Primary Goal**: Make the contrast between past struggle and present mastery undeniably visceral.
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 2.5s`: **The Problem State (Before)**. Desaturated, slow, friction-heavy, disorganized.
  - `2.5s – 3.5s`: **The Catalyst / Wipe**. Physical wipe line, laser sweep, or water wash across the screen.
  - `3.5s – 8.0s`: **The Transformed State (After)**. Vibrant saturation, instant speed, frictionless elegance.
  - `8.0s – 12.0s`: **Side-by-Side Dual Analysis**. Split-screen metric comparison (+300% speed, -80% stress).
- **Recommended Engine**: `remotion-dev`, `hand-drawn-canvas-animation`, `animate`.

---

## 08. The Testimonial & Social Proof
- **Primary Goal**: Convert skepticism into trust through concrete, undeniable real-world results.
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 2.0s`: **The Outrageous Number / Quote**. "We generated $140,000 in 14 days without ads."
  - `2.0s – 6.0s`: **The Initial Obstacle**. Quick verification of the starting point / disbelief.
  - `6.0s – 11.0s`: **The System in Action**. Animated analytics dashboard or client message proof.
  - `11.0s – 15.0s`: **Direct Endorsement & Next Step**.
- **Recommended Engine**: `video-edit`, `remotion-motion-graphics`.

---

## 09. The AI Avatar & Digital Mascot Persona
- **Primary Goal**: Scalable personality-driven content while completely sidestepping the uncanny valley.
- **Uncanny Valley Avoidance Strategy**:
  - Commit fully to intentional stylization (pixel art, voxel 3D, or expressive 2D cartoon).
  - Explicit eye blinks, eyebrow twitches, and lip-sync timed directly to audio phonemes.
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 2.0s`: Avatar fourth-wall break (looks directly at camera, blinks, taps screen).
  - `2.0s – 10.0s`: Avatar presents numbered step cards with animated gestures.
  - `10.0s – 15.0s`: Friendly wave / sign-off with clear call to action.
- **Recommended Engine**: `kinetic`, `mascota-animada`, `mascota-pixel`, `becario-reels`.

---

## 10. The Podcast & Audio-Visual Transformation
- **Primary Goal**: Kill the boring static waveform audiogram; transform spoken audio into a cinematic visual journey.
- **Timeline Blueprint**:
  - `0.0s – 2.0s`: **The Quote Impact**. The most provocative statement slams onto screen before speaker audio starts.
  - `2.0s – 8.0s`: **Live Acoustic Kinetic Flow**. Word-by-word active marker highlights + ambient visual metaphor.
  - `8.0s – 14.0s`: **Peak Emotional Climax**. Sound-wave particle field or camera push-in matching vocal intensity.
  - `14.0s – 16.0s`: Episode / show branding badge.
- **Recommended Engine**: `kinetic`, `hand-drawn-canvas-animation`, `remotion-motion-graphics`.
